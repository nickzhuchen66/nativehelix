# Capability Guide

Capabilities are reusable knowledge assets rather than automatically executable features.

Each domain may contain:

- `CAPABILITY.md` — domain purpose, scope, assets, and limits;
- `contracts/` — explicit interfaces, records, or decision boundaries;
- `patterns/` — reusable design approaches;
- `checklists/` — inspection and readiness aids;
- `templates/` — starting structures for project-owned artifacts.

Start from `04_Capabilities/INDEX.md`, then load only the domain relevant to your problem. When a Capability refers to another domain, treat that as a dependency to inspect, not permission to load or adopt the entire toolkit.

Capability assets do not carry production maturity into your project. Record the evidence you actually have and validate the mechanism in your environment.
