# Community Code of Conduct

NativeHelix is an open learning and contribution space for practical AI-native product and architecture methods.

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers should:

- be respectful, specific, and constructive;
- critique artifacts and claims rather than people;
- separate evidence, assumptions, and recommendations;
- preserve attribution, limitations, and failure modes;
- welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels;
- protect private information, credentials, and sensitive operational details.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces representing NativeHelix.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer without reposting harmful material. For security or sensitive disclosure concerns, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/nickzhuchen66/nativehelix/security/advisories/new).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate this standard. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of private information.
